Delete Your Account

You can delete your Mahanubhav Sangam account and your data at any time — either directly in the app, or by submitting a request here. This page explains both options and what happens to your information afterward.

Option 1: Delete from within the app

The quickest way to delete your account is from your device:

01

Open Mahanubhav Sangam

Log in and go to your Profile tab.

02

Go to Settings

Tap the settings icon, then select Account.

03

Tap "Delete Account"

Choose Delete Account and confirm your choice when prompted.

04

Confirm

Your profile is removed from search immediately, and account deletion is completed as described below.

Option 2: Request deletion here

If you're unable to access the app, submit a request below or write to us directly, and we'll delete your account and associated data.

Account deletion request

Thank you — we've received your request. We'll verify your details and delete your account within 7 business days, and confirm by email or phone once it's done.

What gets deleted

Once your deletion request is confirmed, we permanently delete or anonymise the following from our active systems:

  • Your profile details — name, date of birth, gender, photographs, and biodata.
  • Contact details — phone number, email address, and city or region.
  • Community details — gotra, Panth lineage, gaddi or math affiliation, and family background.
  • Partner preferences and profile settings.
  • Shortlists, sent and received connection requests, and in-app messages.

What we retain, and for how long

A small amount of information may be retained beyond deletion, only where necessary:

DataRetained forWhy
Basic transaction records (if any payments were made)As required by applicable tax and accounting lawLegal and financial record-keeping
Records of safety reports or complaints involving your accountUp to 12 monthsPlatform safety and fraud prevention
Backups containing your dataUp to 90 daysRemoved during routine backup rotation

Aside from these, we do not keep copies of your profile or personal information after deletion is complete.
