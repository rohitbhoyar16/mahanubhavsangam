About the sangam

A rishta built on shared shraddha

General matrimony platforms treat the Mahanubhav Panth as a checkbox. We built Mahanubhav Sangam because our community's customs, fasts, and Panch-Krishna reverence deserve a platform that understands them from the first screen.

Mahanubhav Sangam connects unmarried sons and daughters of the Mahanubhav Panthiya samaj — spread across Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and beyond — with families who follow the same Achar-Vichar, observe the same Ekadashi and Panchmi traditions, and hold the Leela Charitra close.

Every profile on the app is a family profile first: gotra, Panth lineage, native gaddi or math affiliation, and family background sit alongside age, education, and profession — because for our samaj, a match has always meant two families, not just two people.
